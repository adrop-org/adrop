adrop

Ads that pay the viewer.

Verified humans view, engage and act. Every qualified view pays the viewer in USDC straight from the campaign's escrow: 70% viewer, 20% host app, 10% protocol. No token, no wallet lists.

Try the demo Integrate the SDK Docs

View

An opted-in human watches an ad for the dwell time, on screen and in focus. One on-chain payout per view.

Engage

Attention checks, not clicks. A quiz or an in-ad answer earns a bonus from the same escrow.

Act

An on-chain action or a Blink after the view, paid at a second price. Never a paid click.

Advertisers, human or AI agent, fund a campaign with one HTTP request: POST /campaigns/:id/fund answers 402, the client pays over x402, the server escrows the USDC and the campaign goes live. Advertisers buy verified reach by on-chain behaviour and never receive addresses.
